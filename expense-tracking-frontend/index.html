Expensio Finance — expense tracker, budgets, and shared bills

Expensio Finance is a personal and household expense tracker for daily spending, monthly budgets, recurring bills, and shared costs. It is built for people who want a record they can keep on a weekday, not a spreadsheet they abandon.
